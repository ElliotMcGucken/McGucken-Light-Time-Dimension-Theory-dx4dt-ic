# Introduction: How Foundational Principles Become Physically Real

The question of how a principle becomes *physically real* in physics is not philosophical. It is documentary. The historical record of physics from the seventeenth century forward shows a recurring structural pattern: a single axiom is posited, multiple theorems descend from it, the theorems make empirical predictions across previously disconnected phenomena, and the empirical reach of the predictions establishes the axiom as foundational. The axiom is not proven deductively from a higher principle---no foundational axiom ever has been. It is established by what it does.

Kepler's three laws of planetary motion (the law of elliptical orbits 1609, the equal-areas law 1609, the harmonic law 1619) [@KeplerAstroNova; @KeplerHarmonice] were not proven from anything. They were inferred from Tycho Brahe's observational data and posited as the structural content of planetary motion. Their physical reality accumulated through their empirical reach: they replaced the Ptolemaic and Copernican circular-orbit frameworks because they made empirically successful predictions across all six known planets of the Solar System.

Newton's *Principia* of 1687 [@NewtonPrincipia] did not prove gravitation. It postulated the inverse-square law of universal gravitation and the three laws of motion as axioms, then derived Kepler's three laws as theorems, along with the tides, the precession of the equinoxes, the trajectories of comets, the oblateness of the Earth, and the figure of the lunar orbit. The empirical reach across these previously disconnected phenomena established gravitation as physically real over the subsequent two centuries.

Maxwell's 1865 *Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field* [@Maxwell1865] did not prove electromagnetic waves. It postulated the displacement current and the four field equations as axioms, then derived a wave equation with propagation speed (μ₀ ε₀)^{-1/2} numerically matching Fizeau's measurement of c. The structural identification *light is an electromagnetic wave* was a theoretical consequence of the postulate, confirmed empirically by Hertz in 1887 [@Hertz1887] when he generated and detected radio waves at the predicted speed.

Einstein's 1905 photoelectric paper [@Einstein1905photoelectric] did not prove quantization. It postulated that light energy comes in discrete packets E = hν as an axiom, then derived the frequency-dependence of the photoelectric effect that Lenard had observed and that classical electromagnetism could not explain. The empirical reach extended through Compton scattering [@Compton1923], the photoelectric work-function measurements [@MillikanPhotoelectric1916], blackbody spectra, and atomic spectral lines, with the physical reality of the quantum accumulating across the subsequent two decades until quantization became the foundational content of quantum mechanics.

The pattern is exact across all four cases. An axiom is posited. Theorems descend. Empirical reach establishes physical reality. No deductive proof from a higher principle is offered, because the axiom *is* the higher principle---it sits at the foundational layer.

The McGucken Principle dx₄/dt = ic [@LTDTheory; @MdgUNC1998; @MdgFQXi2008] has the same structural status. It is posited as a single axiom: *the fourth dimension is expanding at the velocity of light c in a spherically symmetric manner from every spacetime event p of the four-dimensional manifold*. From this axiom descend the Lorentz invariance of the light cone, the Tsirelson saturation of Bell-inequality violation, the Born rule, the Schrödinger equation, the Dirac equation, the canonical commutation relations, Huygens' self-replication of wavefronts, the Feynman path integral, the First Law of Nonlocality, the Schwarzschild solution, the Bekenstein--Hawking entropy, the Standard Model phenomenology at PDG precision, the resolution of the nine Sorkin/Martín-Martínez QFT measurement pathologies, the thermodynamic arrows of time, and the twistor and amplituhedron structures [@MdgGRQM; @MdgSphereFoundationalAtom; @MdgLagrangian; @SphereUniqueness].

This is a wider empirical reach from a single axiom than Newton, Kepler, Maxwell, or Einstein achieved in their founding papers.
