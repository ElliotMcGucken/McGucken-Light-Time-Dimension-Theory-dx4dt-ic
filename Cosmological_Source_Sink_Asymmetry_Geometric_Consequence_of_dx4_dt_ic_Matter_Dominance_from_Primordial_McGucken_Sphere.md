# Background and Statement of Result

## The McGucken Principle and the Spherical Advance

The McGucken Principle is the local geometric statement
$$\frac{dx₄}{dt} = ic,
\label{eq:mcgucken}$$
where x₄ is the geometric coordinate of x₄-advance, c is the invariant speed, and i is selected by the Frobenius theorem as the unique element of ℂ preserving magnitude while squaring to -1 [@McGucken2026Sphere; @McGucken2026iAsGenerator]. The principle fires at every event of the four-fold ontology, generating an outgoing spherical x₄-wavefront --- the McGucken Sphere of radius ct around each source event.

The four-fold ontology distinguishes four kinematic regimes:

1.  Absolute rest in x₁ x₂ x₃: a massive particle at spatial rest spends its full four-velocity budget into x₄-advance.

2.  Absolute rest in x₄: a photon at v = c, with dx₄/dt = 0 on its null worldline, riding the wavefront.

3.  Absolute motion: the spherical x₄-expansion at ic from every event.

4.  The CMB frame: the isotropic cosmological x₄-expansion direction.

## The Source--Sink Duality

At any field point P, the wave equation generated by [\[eq:mcgucken\]](#eq:mcgucken){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mcgucken"} admits a Green's-function decomposition into retarded and advanced kernels. The retarded kernel propagates outgoing wavefronts from P into its future x₄-light-cone; the advanced kernel sums incoming wavefronts arriving at P from its past x₄-light-cone. Both kernels solve the same wave equation, and their combination is the Feynman propagator.

In the LTD reading, the same physical x₄-field at P thus admits two equivalent local descriptions:

::: definition
**Definition 1** (Source and sink readings). At a field point P in x₄-advance:

-   The *outgoing-source reading* treats P as a source of spherical x₄-wavefronts propagating into P's future x₄-light-cone.

-   The *incoming-sink reading* treats P as the convergence locus of spherical x₄-wavefronts arriving from events in P's past x₄-light-cone.
:::

Both readings are exact at the level of the wave equation. They are not separate fields; they are dual descriptions of the same field, related by the substitution i ↔ -i in [\[eq:mcgucken\]](#eq:mcgucken){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mcgucken"}.

::: theorem
**Theorem 1** (Geometric matter-antimatter asymmetry). *Let ψ be a Dirac spinor field on the x₄-advance geometry generated by [\[eq:mcgucken\]](#eq:mcgucken){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:mcgucken"}. Statements (i)--(iv) together constitute the geometric content of the matter-antimatter asymmetry: it is structural (not mechanism-driven), it is preserved under local CPT, and it is overwhelming at primordial epoch.

# Local Duality and Cosmological Non-Duality

## Local Source--Sink Duality

At any single point P in the interior of x₄-advance --- meaning P has both a nonempty past x₄-light-cone and a nonempty future x₄-light-cone --- the source and sink readings are exactly dual. The set of past events sourcing wavefronts that converge at P is, up to time-reversal, the set of future events into which P sources wavefronts. The wave equation does not distinguish them; the Green's function decomposition is exact.

::: proposition
**Proposition 2** (Local CPT symmetry). *For any point P in the interior of x₄-advance with V_{\text{past}}(P) > 0 and V_{\text{future}}(P) > 0, the local production rates of \psi₊ and \psi₋ excitations at P are equal.*
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* Local production at P is governed by vertex amplitudes computed in the Feynman propagator. The propagator is CPT-symmetric. Therefore the production rates of \psi₊ and \psi₋ at P are equal. ◻
:::

This recovers the standard prediction: at any laboratory epoch, pair production from photons yields equal numbers of \psi₊ and \psi₋ excitations. Antimatter is produced as readily as matter, in equal numbers, when there is a fully populated past x₄-light-cone available.

## Cosmological Non-Duality

The cosmological situation is structurally different. Let t = 0 denote the initial event and t > 0 the cosmological x₄-advance time. Then:

-   The future x₄-light-cone from any event at cosmological time t is unbounded --- it includes all of cosmological history after t.

-   The past x₄-light-cone from any event at cosmological time t is bounded --- it has volume at most V_{\text{past}}(t) ∼ t⁴ (in natural units with c = 1), and at t = 0 it has zero volume.

The two readings are therefore not cosmologically dual:

::: theorem
**Theorem 2** (Cosmological asymmetry of readings). *Let \mathcal{V}_{\text{src}}(t) denote the past x₄-volume available to source outgoing wavefronts converging at a generic field point at cosmological time t, and \mathcal{V}_{\text{snk}}(t) the past x₄-volume available to populate the incoming-sink reading. Then \mathcal{V}_{\text{src}}(t) = \mathcal{V}_{\text{snk}}(t) at the level of the bare wave equation, but the cosmological boundary condition makes the *net* contribution asymmetric: the outgoing-source reading is supplied by the cosmological x₄-expansion itself (the McGucken Sphere of the initial event, which is the dominant feature of the geometry at all t), while the incoming-sink reading is supplied only by the locally available past reservoir, which at small t is negligible.*
:::

::: proof
*Sketch of proof.* The McGucken Sphere of the initial event has radius ct at cosmological time t and dominates the global x₄-field structure. It is by construction an outgoing structure --- the spherical x₄-advance is net outgoing from the initial event. At any field point P at cosmological time t, the outgoing-source reading at P includes a contribution from the cosmological-scale outgoing flow, while the incoming-sink reading at P draws only on locally available past events whose own outgoing wavefronts converge at P.

At small t, the local past reservoir has volume ∼ t⁴ → 0, so the sink-reading contribution vanishes. At large t, the local past reservoir grows, but the cosmological-scale outgoing flow continues to dominate because it is sourced by the singular initial event and its first-epoch descendants. The ratio \mathcal{V}_{\text{snk}}(t) / \mathcal{V}_{\text{src,net}}(t) is therefore bounded above by a function of t that vanishes at t = 0 and grows monotonically but remains less than unity.

Full quantitative analysis is deferred to the companion calculation paper. ◻
:::

## Primordial Domination

The single sharpest qualitative consequence is the primordial epoch:

::: theorem
**Theorem 3** (Primordial matter domination). *At t → 0⁺, the population ratio N₋(t)/N₊(t) → 0. All early-epoch spinor excitations couple to the outgoing-source reading because the incoming-sink reading has no past reservoir to draw from.*
:::

::: proof
*Proof.* At cosmological time t, the past x₄-light-cone volume at any field point is bounded above by V_{\text{past}}(t) ≤ \frac{π²}{2}(ct)⁴. As t → 0⁺, V_{\text{past}}(t) → 0, so the incoming-sink reading at every field point has vanishing support. The outgoing-source reading, in contrast, is supplied by the initial event itself, which has finite (and in fact maximal) source content. Therefore all spinor excitations at t → 0⁺ couple to the outgoing-source reading, giving \psi₊ excitations (matter) and not \psi₋ excitations (antimatter). ◻
:::

::: remark
*Remark 1*. This is the central physical content of the paper: matter is the spinor representation excited by the primordial x₄-expansion, and antimatter as a cosmological population is not suppressed by a mechanism --- it is geometrically excluded at the epoch where the asymmetry is set. The measured CP violation in the kaon and B-meson systems (Cronin--Fitch 1964; BaBar and Belle B-meson asymmetries) is consistent with this picture. ALPHA-g (2023) confirmed antihydrogen falls under gravity with g\bₐr H ≈ gH at ∼ 20\% precision. This is consistent: the gravitational coupling sees the spinor representation's stress-energy, which is CPT-symmetric. The asymmetry is in population, not in coupling.

# Toward a Quantitative Calculation: The +ic Dynamics as C-Breaker

## Methodological Statement and Replacement of the Earlier Vertex-Asymmetry Framing

The structural argument of Sections 1--5 establishes that a primordial matter-antimatter asymmetry follows from the geometry of dx₄/dt = ic. It does not by itself produce a numerical value for \etaB ≡ (nB - n\bₐr B)/(nB + n\bₐr B) ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰.

An earlier framing of this calculation interpreted the asymmetry as arising from differential coupling of matter and antimatter spinors at a pair-production vertex with the cosmological-scale x₄-flow inserted as an external classical field. That framing is incorrect, and we retract it here. The reason is the QED paper's explicit derivation [@McGucken2026QED], Section IV.4: the gauge connection A_μ in LTD-derived QED couples vectorially to \bar\psiγ^μψ, which under charge conjugation behaves as \bar\psiγ^μψ → -\bar\psiγ^μψ together with A_μ → -A_μ, leaving the local vertex interaction Lagrangian C-invariant. The Compton-scattering verification in Section IX of [@McGucken2026QED] reproduces the Klein-Nishina formula exactly, with no C-violating term. Local LTD-QED amplitudes are C-symmetric and CPT-symmetric to the same precision as standard QED. No vertex-level mechanism within the local Feynman rules can produce \etaB \ne 0.

The correct location of the C-asymmetry is the global dynamical structure of the +ic direction itself. Postulate 1 specifies dx₄/dt = +ic with the positive sign --- the McGucken Sphere expands, it does not contract --- and this + sign is a feature of the *dynamics*, not of a boundary condition. The +ic direction is present at every event of x₄-advance throughout cosmological history; it pervades every propagator (the +iε prescription as the algebraic signature of +ic, [@McGucken2026QED] Section VII.3, [@McGucken2026FeynmanDiagrams] Proposition III.2), every vertex (the i as perpendicularity marker, [@McGucken2026FeynmanDiagrams] Proposition IV.1), and the global structure of the x₄-orientation bundle (the +ic direction as global section, [@McGucken2026QED] Section VIII.3). Local amplitudes remain C-symmetric because the |iε| and |i| structure is C-neutral as a formal regulator, but the integrated cosmological history of x₄-advance is biased: the +ic direction co-aligns with the matter orientation sector (k > 0 in condition (M) of [@McGucken2026Dirac; @McGucken2026SecondQuant]) and anti-aligns with the antimatter sector (k < 0).

This is structurally identical to how the second law arises in LTD [@McGucken2026Thermodynamics]: the asymmetric dynamics dx₄/dt = +ic directly produces entropy increase through the monotonic spherical expansion of phase-space volume from every event. There is no Past Hypothesis in LTD; the second law is a theorem of the asymmetric dynamics, not a consequence of a postulated low-entropy initial condition. The matter-antimatter asymmetry developed here follows the same structural pattern: the asymmetric +ic dynamics, integrated over cosmological history, directly produces the population asymmetry between orientation sectors. Neither phenomenon requires a separate initial-condition postulate; both arise from the single dynamical fact that +ic is the dynamics and -ic is not.

This is also structurally parallel to the monopole-absence theorem of [@McGucken2026QED], Section VIII.3: the same global +ic direction that provides the dynamical + sign provides a global section of the x₄-orientation U(1) bundle, forcing bundle triviality and excluding monopoles. Three apparently separate phenomena --- the second law (entropy increase), the absence of magnetic monopoles, and the matter-antimatter asymmetry \etaB \ne 0 --- are three manifestations of the same dynamical root: the + sign in dx₄/dt = +ic, which Postulate 1 distinguishes from the alternative -ic.

We approach the numerical question with explicit acknowledgement of the free parameters involved. The standard for honesty here is the same standard used in inflationary cosmology: slow-roll inflation requires specifying the inflaton potential shape, the initial field value, the number of e-folds, the reheating temperature, and the inflaton coupling to Standard Model fields, all of which are free parameters tuned post hoc to observation. The honest scientific practice is not to claim zero free parameters but to state every parameter explicitly and not bury any inside the prose. We do that here.

## Geometric Setup

Let P be a generic field point at cosmological x₄-advance time t_*, at comoving radial coordinate r from the initial event, with r < R_* ≡ c t_* (i.e., P lies inside the McGucken Sphere of the initial event).

The asymmetric population at P arises from two contributions to the integrated cosmological dynamics, distinguished by their coupling to the global +ic direction:

1.  *Cosmological-scale outgoing flow:* the McGucken Sphere of the initial event has expanded to radius R_* by time t_*, and the +ic direction of the dynamics is co-aligned with the outgoing-source reading of the x₄-field at every point inside this sphere [@McGucken2026FeynmanDiagrams]. Spinor excitations created during the integrated cosmological history before t_* that ride this outgoing flow populate the k > 0 (matter) orientation sector preferentially, by the co-alignment of +ic with k > 0 in condition (M) of [@McGucken2026Dirac; @McGucken2026SecondQuant]. The amplitude at P from this cosmological-scale dynamical structure, by 3-D Huygens kernel falloff on the McGucken Sphere of the initial event, is
    $A_{\text{cosmo}}(P, t_*) ∼ \frac{S_{\text{init}}}{R_*²},$
    where S_{\text{init}} is the (dimensionful) cosmological-scale source strength and the 1/R_*² scaling is the standard 3-D Huygens amplitude falloff at distance R_*.

2.  *C-symmetric local pair production:* at every field point with a populated past light cone, local C-symmetric Feynman-rule pair production from ambient photon flux populates both orientation sectors equally. The amplitude at the local correlation scale \ell_* is
    $A_{\text{local}}(P, t_*) ∼ \frac{S_{\text{loc}}}{\ell_*²}.$
    This contribution is C-symmetric: it produces equal k > 0 and k < 0 populations.

Treating the source strengths S_{\text{init}} and S_{\text{loc}} as comparable per unit source event (the natural assumption in the absence of a derivation distinguishing them; this is a sub-assumption of P3), the primordial asymmetry parameter at t_* is the ratio of the C-asymmetric to the total contribution:
$$\etaB(t_*) = \frac{A_{\text{cosmo}}}{A_{\text{cosmo}} + A_{\text{local}}} = \frac{1/R_*²}{1/R_*² + 1/\ell_*²} = \frac{\ell_*²}{\ell_*² + R_*²}.
\label{eq:eta-master}$$

For \ell_* \ll R_* (which holds in all physical regimes of interest),
$$\etaB(t_*) ≈ \left(\frac{\ell_*}{R_*}\right)².
\label{eq:eta-squared}$$

This is the master formula. Its symbolic content is rigorous given P3 (the 3-D Huygens-amplitude reading from [@McGucken2026FeynmanDiagrams]). Its numerical content depends on P1 and P2.

## Numerical Estimates

We compute \etaB for two natural choices of P1, with P2 taken in each case as the thermal de Broglie wavelength.

**Case A (Electroweak freeze-out):** T_* = 100 GeV.

$$\begin{aligned}
t_* &∼ 10⁻¹⁰ \text{ s} \quad \text{(standard cosmology)}, \\
R_* &= c t_* ∼ 3 × 10⁻² \text{ m}, \\
\ell_* &= ℏ/(kB T_*) ∼ 2 × 10⁻¹⁸ \text{ m}, \\
\etaB^{\text{(A)}} &≈ \left(\frac{2 × 10⁻¹⁸}{3 × 10⁻²}\right)² ≈ 4 × 10⁻³³.
\end{aligned}$$

**Case B (GUT-scale freeze-out):** T_* = 10¹⁶ GeV.

$$\begin{aligned}
t_* &∼ 10⁻³⁸ \text{ s}, \\
R_* &∼ 3 × 10⁻³⁰ \text{ m}, \\
\ell_* &∼ 2 × 10⁻³² \text{ m}, \\
\etaB^{\text{(B)}} &≈ \left(\frac{2 × 10⁻³²}{3 × 10⁻³⁰}\right)² ≈ 4 × 10⁻⁵.
\end{aligned}$$

**Observed value:** \etaB^{\text{obs}} ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰.

## Honest Assessment of the Numerical Result

The observed \etaB ≈ 6 × 10⁻¹⁰ lies within the bracket [4 × 10⁻³³, 4 × 10⁻⁵] generated by the two natural choices of freeze-out scale. The observed value corresponds, under the squared-scaling [\[eq:eta-squared\]](#eq:eta-squared){reference-type="eqref" reference="eq:eta-squared"}, to a freeze-out energy scale of approximately
$T_*^{\text{fit}} ∼ 10².⁵ \text{ TeV} ≈ 300 \text{ TeV},$
i.e., intermediate between the electroweak scale and the GUT scale. This is suggestive but not predictive: we are not deriving T_* from independent geometric considerations, so any value in the bracket can be obtained by adjusting P1.

The 28-order-of-magnitude spread between Case A and Case B reflects the sensitivity of \etaB to T_* under the squared scaling. This sensitivity is itself a real prediction of the framework: the observed \etaB should correspond to a specific freeze-out energy, and that energy --- approximately 10².⁵ TeV --- is a falsifiable target. If the eventual independent determination of T_* (from LTD freeze-out geometry rather than Standard Model thermal history) gives a value far from 10².⁵ TeV, the framework fails this test.

The functional form P3 (squared scaling from 3-D Huygens propagation) is the substantive theoretical commitment. Alternative scalings (e.g., logarithmic from infrared physics, or linear from 1-D effective propagation along a privileged direction) give different brackets:

-   Linear scaling \etaB ≈ \ell_*/R_*: gives \etaB^{\text{(A)}} ≈ 7 × 10⁻¹⁷, \etaB^{\text{(B)}} ≈ 7 × 10⁻³, with the observed value at T_*^{\text{fit}} ∼ 1 TeV.
