## 12. Supersymmetry

### 12.1 Statement

**Theorem 11.1 (Supersymmetry as algebraic extension of the McGucken Algebra).** *Supersymmetry, if realised in Nature, is the unique non-trivial graded-Lie-algebra extension of 𝔦𝔰𝔬(1,3) consistent with the Coleman-Mandula theorem and its supersymmetric extension by Haag-Łopuszański-Sohnius. ### 12.2 Channel A: Algebraic Derivation

The Coleman-Mandula theorem states that, under reasonable assumptions, the only Lie-algebraic symmetries of the S-matrix in d ≥ 3 are 𝔦𝔰𝔬(1,3) times internal symmetries. If supersymmetry is realised, it is a Channel-A extension; if not, the McGucken framework is intact.

The geometric content of supersymmetry, if realised, is superspace: an extension of ℝ¹,³ by Grassmann-valued coordinates θ^α, θ̄^α̇. Superfields Φ(x, θ, θ̄) realise the supersymmetric algebra geometrically as translations in superspace.

### 12.4 Channel Synthesis

**Proposition 11.2 (Channel synthesis for supersymmetry).** *Channel A (graded extension of 𝔦𝔰𝔬(1,3)) and Channel B (superspace as Grassmann-extended Minkowski) describe the same supersymmetric structure, joined by the shared invariant: the four-momentum P_μ, which is on both sides the McGucken-generated translation generator.*

**Remark 11.3.** Whether supersymmetry is realised in Nature is an empirical question. The McGucken framework neither requires it nor excludes it: it accommodates supersymmetry as an extension if found, and is complete without it if not.
