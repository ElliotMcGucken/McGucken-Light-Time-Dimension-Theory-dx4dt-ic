### The Lemma on the Uniqueness of the Rotation-Invariant Probability Measure on the Sphere.

    F(x) := ∫_(SO(3)) f(gx) dm(g), x ∈ S² .

For every rotation h, F(hx) = ∫ f(ghx) dm(g) = F(x) by the right-invariance of m. The group SO(3) acts transitively on S², so F is a constant C_f that depends only on f. By the invariance of μ, ∫ f(x) dμ(x) = ∫ f(gx) dμ(x) for every g. Integrating over g with respect to m, and exchanging the order of integration by Fubini's theorem (f is continuous and bounded), gives

    ∫_(S²) f dμ = ∫_(S²) F dμ = C_f .

The measure σ/4π is also a rotation-invariant probability measure, so the same computation gives ∫ f dσ/4π = C_f. The two measures agree on every continuous function, and by the Riesz representation theorem they are equal. **QED.** The general theorem, for homogeneous spaces of locally compact groups, is given in [95].

## Era I — The Princeton Origin

The McGucken Principle traces directly to undergraduate work at Princeton under John Archibald Wheeler. Two projects planted the seeds: deriving the time factor of the Schwarzschild metric by "poor man's reasoning," and work on the EPR paradox and delayed-choice experiments with Joseph Taylor. McGucken's theory appears as an appendix in his 1998 Ph.D. dissertation.

## Era II — First Internet Deployments

By the early 2000s the theory appeared online as Moving Dimensions Theory and later Dynamic Dimensions Theory. Usenet posts developed the core argument that Einstein's postulates follow as theorems from the single fact dx₄/dt = ic.

## Era III — The Heroic Age of Forum Debates

The first indexed, peer-visible statement of the theory appeared in the 2008 FQXi essay "Time as an Emergent Phenomenon: Traveling Back to the Heroic Age of Physics." Further essays through 2013 refined the framework and expanded its reach. McGucken also developed the theory across books, blogs, and online communities, including [*Light Time Dimension Theory*](https://www.amazon.com/Light-Time-Dimension-Theory-Foundational/dp/B0D2NNN6PW/) and [*The Physics of Time*](https://www.amazon.com/Physics-Time-Mechanics-Relativity-Thermodynamics/dp/B0F2PZCW6B/) on Amazon, essays on [Medium](https://goldennumberratio.medium.com/) and [Substack](https://elliotmcgucken.substack.com/), and discussion in the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/movingdimensions/).

## Era IV — Books and the McGucken Principle

Book publications, blogs, Medium essays, and later [elliotmcguckenphysics.com](https://elliotmcguckenphysics.com/) consolidated the theory under the Light Time Dimension Theory and McGucken Principle framework. From 2025 onward, this program expanded into formal derivations spanning relativity, quantum theory, thermodynamics, field theory, and cosmology.
