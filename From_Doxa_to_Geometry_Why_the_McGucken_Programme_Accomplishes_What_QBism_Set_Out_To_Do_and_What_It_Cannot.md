## I. Statement of the QBist Program

QBism, as formulated canonically by Fuchs and Schack (2013) and elaborated by Mermin (2014) and the DeBrota–Fuchs–Stacey corpus (2020–2024), rests on the following load-bearing claims:

**Q1. (Doxastic reading of the state.)** The quantum state |*ψ*⟩, or equivalently the density operator *ρ*, is not a property of a system. It is an agent's personal degree of belief about the outcomes of measurements the agent might choose to perform. Two agents may legitimately assign different states to the same system without either being wrong.

**Q2. (Born rule as normative.)** The Born rule is not a physical law in the sense of *F = ma*. It is a coherence constraint of the same logical type as the probability axioms: an agent who refuses to use it when betting on quantum outcomes is exposed to a Dutch book. The "only a little more" beyond Bayesian coherence is empirical — the world happens to admit SIC-POVMs (where they exist) — but the rule itself is a rule of thought.

**Q3. (Measurement is personal experience.)** A measurement is not a physical interaction between system and apparatus that produces a definite outcome via a mysterious collapse. It is the agent taking an action and undergoing an experience. The "outcome" is whatever the agent experiences; the state update is the agent's revision of their beliefs.

**Q4. (Participatory realism.)** Reality is not a third-person tableau independent of agents. Agents are constitutive of facts about their own experiences. Quantum mechanics is the manual for navigating a world in which agents participate rather than spectate.

**Q5. (The Urgleichung.)** Where SIC-POVMs exist in dimension *d*, the Born rule can be rewritten as a relation among probabilities only — no Hilbert space vectors, no operators — of the form

  *Q*(*j*) = ∑_*i* [(d+1) *P*(*i*) − 1/d] *r*(*j*|*i*)

connecting hypothetical SIC-reference probabilities *P*(*i*), hypothetical conditional probabilities *r*(*j*|*i*), and the agent's actual betting probabilities *Q*(*j*) for an arbitrary subsequent measurement.

**Q6. (Anti-realism about the wavefunction; agnosticism about the world.)** QBism takes no position on the ontology of the world *behind* the agent's experience. It explicitly refuses the metaphysical project. The world is whatever calls forth the agent's experiences when the agent acts; saying more is, in Fuchs's words, ambiguity all the way down.

These are the claims as the QBists make them. The remainder of this paper assesses (i) which problems Q1–Q6 actually solve, (ii) which they leave unsolved by design, and (iii) which the McGucken Principle *dx*₄/*dt* = *ic* solves outright.


## II. What QBism Genuinely Accomplishes

It is intellectually obligatory to credit QBism's real achievements before discussing its limits.

**A1. Dissolution of the wavefunction-objectification error.** The standard textbook treats |*ψ*⟩ as a thing in the world that "collapses." This generates the Measurement Problem in its hardest form: when, exactly, and by what physical mechanism, does an isolated unitary evolution become a discontinuous projection? QBism's answer — that |*ψ*⟩ was never a thing in the world, so there is nothing to collapse — is a clean dissolution rather than a solution. The Measurement Problem as classically posed presupposes the realist reading of the state; reject the reading and the problem evaporates. This is real philosophical work.

**A2. Diagnosis of the Wigner's-Friend and EPR pseudo-paradoxes.** If states are personal, then Wigner and his Friend assigning different states to the same lab is no paradox; it is two agents with different information. Similarly, the apparent superluminal "influence" in EPR is dissolved: Alice's state update on Bob's particle is *Alice's* belief revision, not a physical signal propagating through space. QBism correctly localises the "spooky" element to the agent's epistemic books, not to a non-local physical channel.

**A3. Stimulation of the SIC-POVM mathematics.** The QBist research program drove substantial new mathematical work on symmetric informationally complete POVMs (Zauner's conjecture, the algebraic number theory of fiducial vectors, the Heisenberg–Weyl orbit construction). This is genuine mathematics, useful well beyond foundations, in quantum key distribution and entanglement detection. The *Urgleichung* is a real reformulation of the Born rule, even where one disputes its interpretation.

**A4. Forcing honesty about the role of the agent.** Bohr gestured at the indispensability of the classical observer; QBism makes that gesture precise as a normative claim about belief revision. Whether one accepts the doxastic ontology or not, QBism's insistence that one cannot make sense of a quantum measurement without locating *someone* for whom an outcome occurs is correct.

**A5. Cleanliness about probability.** Bayesian subjectivism (de Finetti, Ramsey, Savage) is a coherent and well-developed account of probability. QBism's choice to read quantum probabilities as such probabilities is consistent and avoids the philosophical contortions of frequentism or propensity-theoretic readings.

These five accomplishments are not minor. Any serious successor program must preserve them — must dissolve the same pseudo-paradoxes, must respect the agent's role, must be at least as clean about probability. The McGucken Programme does preserve them, and the demonstration is given in §V below. But first, the silences.


## III. What QBism Does Not Accomplish (By Design and By Default)

QBism's proponents are explicit that the program is silent on most of what physics has historically asked of an interpretation. This is sometimes presented as humility. We treat each silence on its merits.

### III.1. The Hilbert-space dimension *d*

QBism takes the dimension *d* of the system's Hilbert space as input. It does not derive it. For a qubit, *d* = 2 is a postulate; for a hydrogen atom, *d* = ∞ is a postulate. The agent must be told, from outside the theory, what dimensional structure to use when assigning beliefs.

This is not a small lacuna. The classical question "why is the electron's spin two-valued?" reappears in QBist clothing as "why must the agent's beliefs about Stern–Gerlach outcomes live in *d* = 2?" — and is given no answer.

### III.2. The values of *c* and *ℏ*

Neither the speed of light nor Planck's constant appears anywhere in the foundational QBist axioms. Both must be imported from outside as empirical inputs. The QBist agent has beliefs that satisfy a normative coherence rule; nothing in the rule explains why *ℏ* = 1.054 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s rather than some other value, or why *c* is finite, or why they bear the dimensional relationships they do to mass, length, and time.

### III.3. The Schrödinger equation

QBism does not derive the Schrödinger equation. It accepts the equation as part of the apparatus an agent uses for belief revision between measurements. The first-order time derivative, the factor of *i*, the Hamiltonian generator — all of these are taken on board as empirically successful structure. QBism explains neither (i) why the dynamical equation is first-order in time, (ii) why *i* appears, nor (iii) what the Hamiltonian *is*, ontologically, if states are degrees of belief and the world has been declared off-limits.

### III.4. The Dirac equation, relativity, the Lorentz group

QBism is non-relativistic in its foundational presentation. Relativistic extension is treated as a future task. Lorentz invariance, the Dirac equation, the existence of antimatter, spinor structure, and the connection between spin and statistics are all outside the scope of the doxastic reformulation. Fuchs's "participatory realism" gestures at consistency with relativity but provides no derivation of the Lorentz group, no account of why spacetime has signature (−,+,+,+), and no explanation of the connection between *c* and the structure of the agent's beliefs.

### III.5. Gravity

QBism has no account of gravity. The General Theory of Relativity sits beside the doxastic reformulation as an unexplained, externally-given theory of a classical metric field. Whether the metric is itself an object of agential belief, whether geometric curvature is a property of the world (if there is one) or of the agent's bookkeeping, is unanswered. There is no QBist gravity research program comparable to the QBist SIC-POVM program.

### III.6. The Second Law and the arrows of time

QBism is silent on thermodynamics. The Second Law, the cosmological arrow of time, the radiative arrow, the psychological arrow, the quantum-measurement arrow — all of these are external givens. The doxastic reformulation does not explain why entropy increases, why the past is recordable and the future is not, or why the agent's experiences are temporally ordered.

### III.7. Cosmology

QBism has no cosmology. The CMB, dark energy, large-scale structure, the value of *H*₀, the Hubble tension, BAO, supernova distance moduli, and the apparent fine-tuning of the universe for the existence of agents lie wholly outside the program. There is no QBist analogue of an FRW metric and no derivation of cosmological parameters from doxastic principles.

### III.8. The existence of a measurement event

This is the deepest silence. QBism replaces "measurement" with "agent's experience." The replacement is grammatically clean but ontologically empty: it gives no account of what an "experience" is, when one occurs, what its physical correlates are, or why some interactions (a photon hitting a detector) count as occasioning an experience while others (a photon traversing a beam splitter) do not. The "Heisenberg cut" is relocated from the system–apparatus boundary to the agent–world boundary and then declared not to require a physical explanation. The Measurement Problem is dissolved by being renamed. (See Bacciagaluppi 2014 for the formal version of this objection: QBism's normative Born rule presupposes the very probabilities of actual measurement outcomes that the rule purports to constrain.)

### III.9. Why *the SIC-POVMs exist*

The *Urgleichung* (§I, Q5) is conditional: *if* SIC-POVMs exist in every finite dimension, *then* the agent's beliefs can be coordinatised this way. Zauner's conjecture (SIC existence in all *d*) remains open. QBism gives no physical reason why the world should be such that SICs exist; it observes that, where they do, the formalism is unusually clean.

### III.10. The connection between agent and world

This is the meta-silence. QBism's "participatory realism" tells us that the world is whatever calls forth experience when the agent acts. It does not say what the world is. It does not say why agents exist, why they are spatially localised, why they age in one temporal direction, why their experiences exhibit the particular structure (SIC-coordinatisable beliefs) that they do, or how multiple agents coordinate (as they manifestly do — physics is a public discipline). Wigner-Friend scenarios are *dissolved* at the level of state assignment, but the question of how Alice and Bob's reports about the lab come to agree intersubjectively is, again, declared outside scope.

These ten silences are not failures relative to a misconceived standard. They are failures relative to the standard that interpretations of quantum mechanics have historically been required to meet — the standard that *some* account, even a programmatic one, must be given of how the formalism connects to the world it apparently describes. QBism's answer — that there is no world independent of agents, or that if there is, we have nothing systematic to say about it — is a renunciation, not a resolution.

## VIII. Standing Objections Considered

### VIII.1. "Doesn't the McGucken Programme just objectify the wavefunction in a new guise?"

No. The geometric object — the McGucken Sphere — is the active expansion *dx*₄/*dt* = *ic*, which is a propagation, not a thing. The mode amplitude |*ψ*⟩ is, as in QBism, not a thing in the world; but unlike QBism, what the world *is* (the four-dimensional active expansion) is specified rather than declared off-limits. The McGucken Programme objectifies the *geometry*, not the wavefunction. QBism's correct critique of wavefunction realism is preserved.

### VIII.2. "Doesn't this reintroduce a preferred frame and violate relativity?"

The CMB frame is preferred *cosmologically*, not locally. Local Lorentz invariance is preserved at the tangent-space level (M4 in §IV). This is consistent with observation: there *is* a preferred cosmological frame (the CMB), and there *is* local Lorentz invariance. The McGucken Programme reconciles both facts at the cost of treating the CMB frame as fundamental; QBism treats both as external inputs.

### VIII.3. "Hasn't the QBist program shown that we don't need such ontological commitments?"

It has shown that the doxastic ontology is *internally consistent* with the formal predictions of QM. It has not shown that no other ontology accomplishes the same predictions plus the ten further explananda of §III. The McGucken Programme is precisely that further-accomplishing ontology. The question is not whether QBism is internally coherent (it is), but whether its silences are forced or chosen. We have argued that they are chosen, and that an alternative is available.

### VIII.4. "What about Bacciagaluppi's circularity objection?"

Bacciagaluppi (2014) observed that QBism's normative Born rule presupposes the probabilities of actual measurement outcomes that the rule is supposed to constrain. In QBism, this objection bites because the rule is supposed to be *normative only*. In the McGucken Programme, the Born rule is *derived* as a theorem: Theorem 35 of the May 2026 ontic-derivation paper establishes *P*(*x*) = |*ψ*(*x*)|² through Sub-theorems I (complex amplitudes from *dx*₄/*dt* = *ic* via Frobenius rigidity), II (squared modulus by the Cauchy functional equation), II′ (squared modulus by rank-2 Minkowski-metric bilinearity), III (*ψ*\**ψ* as forward-conjugate *x*₄-overlap), and IV (Hilbert space *H* ≅ *L*²(ℝ³, *d*³*x*) as Cauchy completion). There is no circularity, because the rule is not a normative addition to anything; it is a geometric theorem about the rank-2 metric structure that *dx*₄/*dt* = *ic* generates on the spatial slice. The probability of an outcome is computed *before* any betting agent enters the picture, and the betting agent's coherent beliefs are constrained by it for the same reason classical probabilities constrain classical betting beliefs: because the probabilities are facts about the world.

### VIII.5. "Isn't the McGucken Programme just a hidden-variable theory?"

No. It is a wavefront-propagation theory. The "hidden variables" of Bell-type hidden-variable programs are local-realist values for unmeasured quantities, ruled out by the Bell inequalities. The McGucken Programme does not posit such values; it posits a geometric structure (the Sphere) on which the probabilities are projections. Bell-type inequalities are satisfied because the Sphere is non-local in the same sense quantum mechanics is — the projection onto a spatial slice contains correlations across spacelike-separated points by virtue of the joint Sphere geometry. There is no superluminal signal; there is a joint geometric object.

### VIII.6. "Why should we believe this rather than QBism?"

The case for the McGucken Programme over QBism is, at the level of a foundational paper, the following: (i) the Programme accomplishes everything QBism accomplishes (§VII); (ii) the Programme accomplishes ten further things QBism does not (§V); (iii) the Programme has a cosmological fit (McGucken Cosmology paper) that QBism does not pursue; (iv) the Programme makes positive predictions (no graviton; specific BH-entropy structure; preferred CMB frame at cosmological scales) where QBism makes none. The case is not that QBism is wrong about what it says; it is that QBism's silences are not necessary, and that a single geometric postulate discharges them.
