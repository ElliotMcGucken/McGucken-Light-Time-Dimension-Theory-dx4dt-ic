## APPENDIX B

### Physics for Poets: The Law of Moving Dimensions

*(p. 153)*

Einstein's two postulates of relativity state:

> I. The laws of physical phenomena are the same in all inertial reference frames (that is, only the relative motion of inertial frames can be measured: the concept of motion relative to "absolute rest" is meaningless.)
>
> II. The velocity of light (in free space) is a universal constant, independent of any relative motion of the source and the observer.

I propose that these two postulates may be expressed in an alternative manner, by stating the following law of moving dimensions:

> I. The time dimension is moving or expanding relative to the three spatial dimensions.

A proof of this law of moving dimensions is as follows:

According to Einstein's second postulate,

$$\sum_{j=1}^{3} x_j^2 - c^2 t^2 = 0$$

Which can be rewritten as:

$$\sum_{j=1}^{3} x_j^2 = c^2 t^2$$

Which for one dimension becomes:

$$x^2 = c^2 t^2$$

or

$$x = ct$$

Meaning that

$$\frac{dx}{dt} = c$$

Thus it is shown that the spatial and temporal dimensions are moving relative to one another. The laws and equations of quantum mechanics and relativity derive from this fundamental nature of physical reality. Quantum mechanical, statistical, and relativistic phenomena can be accounted by the underlying wave nature of matter's and energy's interaction with moving dimensions which relative motion implies. The fundamental wave nature of all matter and energy derives from the relative motion of space and time. And too, time dialation, relativistic length contraction, and the equivalence of mass and energy can be accounted for.

*(p. 154)*

Wave-particle duality and quantum mechanical probabilistic behavior can be accounted for by the relative motion between the dimensions, which both particles and waves exist in. Feynman's many-path integrals, reflecting the notion that a particle travels all paths, can be accounted for by the fact that until it interacts with matter, a particle has a chance of existing as a pure wave, completely independent of the spatial dimensions. The law of increasing entropy can be accounted for with the fact that all particles have a chance of existing in a dimension which is expanding at a constant rate, equally in all directions, relative to the rest. Einstein's second postulate, stating that the velocity of light is a universal constant, holds to be true because the velocity of light is merely the rate of propagation of a dimension relative to the other dimensions. Although this relative rate of propagations between dimensions may vary, we shall always interpret it as a constant, because we are used to measuring the velocity of the propagation of energy relative to the velocity of the propagation of energy, which we write as *c*.

Relativistic time dialation occurs because as an object approaches the speed of light, an object approaches the speed of the propagation of energy. As all time is measured relative to the propagation of energy, such as the emission of a photon or the occurrence of a random event which liberates energy, less time will pass for an entity which is propagating at a rate which is close to the propagation of energy itself.

*(p. 155)*

As an entity gains velocity, it is rotated into the moving time dimension, and it in a sense catches up with the dimension.

Relativistic length contraction is always accompanied by an increase in velocity, as the probability that each quantum of the object resides in the time dimension is increased. Relativistic length contraction can be accounted for by the fact that as an object gains velocity its probability is rotated into the time dimension, and thus it appears shorter from the perspective of the three spatial dimensions. At the speed of light the object would have zero dimension, and time would stop in its own frame. An entity gains more energy as its velocity increases, and relativity demonstrates that this entity also gains more mass. When energy is added to an entity, it may also appear as mass, as that energy has a finite chance of interacting with the spatial dimensions. All matter has a spatial component, or a probability of interacting with space, whereas a photon only interacts directly with that which is in the time dimension. In order to cause an entity to move, quanta of energy can be added to it, and the entity has a new overall probability that it will be found in the time dimension, which is proportional to the amount of energy which has been added. As only photons can exist purely in the spatial dimension, no entities but for photons can ever reach the speed of light, as all matter has a finite chance of existing purely in the space dimension. This property may give rise to the concept of mass. An entity moves through space time according to its probability of existing in space and time. The more energy a given entity has, the more likely it is to exist in the moving time dimension. Hence its greater velocity, and also its greater chance of interacting with matter over a given distance. This increased chance of interacting with matter over a given distance can be associated with a shorter deBroglie wavelength or a higher frequency. A more energetic photon has a higher frequency, as it is composed of more substance. A more energetic photon has a higher probability of interacting with matter as it passes it by, as its shorter wavelength represents a greater existence in space-time.

A photon has no spatial dimension, as it is matter fully rotated into the time dimension. Einstein's relation which expresses the equivalence between matter and energy (AB.1) holds true because radiative energy, consisting of photons, is

*(p. 156)*

merely matter rotated fully into the time dimension.

$$E = mc^2 \qquad\qquad \text{(AB.1)}$$

In quantum mechanics energy is accounted for by the operator which represents the infinitesimal change with respect to time, while momentum is accounted for by operator which represents the infinitesimal change with respect to space. Both momentum and energy are defined with the concept of change and probability. And too, inherent in all waves are the concepts of motion and probability.

Einstein's first postulate derives from the fact that in all inertial reference frames, the relative motion between the dimensions is fixed at a constant rate. Thus the laws of physics, and all physical concepts, which are all fundamentally based on the concept of motion or change with respect to time, are also fixed.

As physics concerns itself at all levels with changes relative to both space and time, it makes sense that all physics, time, motion, reality, life, and consciousness itself are founded upon a stage which is endowed with intrinsic motion. The underlying fabric of all reality, the dimensions themselves, are moving relative to one another.
