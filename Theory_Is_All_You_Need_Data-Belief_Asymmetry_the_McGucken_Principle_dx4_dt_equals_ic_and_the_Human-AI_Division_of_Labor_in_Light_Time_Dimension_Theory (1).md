# Introduction: The Principle and the Question of Origination

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point. Here $x_4$ is a physical dimension, $c$ is the physical invariant
speed, $t$ is the coordinate time read on physical clocks, and $i$
denotes the physical perpendicularity of the expanding fourth dimension
to the three spatial dimensions $x_1, x_2, x_3$. From this single
postulate the LTD corpus derives, as theorem chains anchored in
established physics, the invariant interval, the Lorentz transformation,
the Wick rotation, the light cone as the Wick-non-invariant shadow of
the McGucken Sphere, the primitive orientation of time carried by the
sign $+ic$, and the common source of quantum mechanics, general
relativity, and the Second Law
[@mcwick; @wicknecessity; @proofsforcing; @twoatom; @sphereatom].

This paper concerns the question of where such a principle can come
from, and what role, if any, artificial intelligence can play in its
discovery, verification, and reception. The occasion is Teppo Felin and
Matthias Holweg's article "Theory Is All You Need: AI, Human Cognition,
and Causal Reasoning," published in *Strategy Science* in December 2024
[@felinholweg]. Felin and Holweg give a general account of why large
language models cannot originate new knowledge and why humans can. The
present paper tests their account against a documented case: the
multi-year collaboration between the author and Anthropic's Claude, in
which every derivation of the LTD corpus was stated, challenged,
defended, machine-checked, and refined. The collaboration record
confirms the Felin--Holweg schema at every point, supplies a live
instance of their central thought experiment, and yields a working
protocol for human--AI collaboration in theoretical physics.

# The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ Reading of Felin and Holweg's Thesis

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point, and the Principle's own history predicts what Felin and Holweg's
thesis requires: a physical postulate held in advance of consensus
support, from which new derivations then flow. Felin and Holweg's
argument runs as follows. Large language models learn from trillions of
tokens of past human text, and their outputs "are based on conditional
probabilities given the structure of the inputs they have encountered in
their training data" [@felinholweg]. Truth, for such a system, "is a
byproduct of statistical patterns and frequencies" [@felinholweg]: a
claim made often in the corpus outweighs a claim made rarely, and
prestige-weighted consensus outweighs a contrarian. Human cognition,
they argue, works by a different mechanism, which they name
*data--belief asymmetry*: the capacity to hold a belief that present
evidence fails to support or actively contradicts, and then to use
theory-based causal reasoning to identify the interventions and
experiments that generate the new data verifying the belief. Their
epigraph-grade authority is Einstein, whom they quote: "It is the theory
which decides what can be observed" [@felinholweg].

Their extended historical case is heavier-than-air flight. The empirical
record through 1903 counted centuries of failed attempts, Otto
Lilienthal's 1896 glider crash, and Samuel Langley's public failures
nine weeks before Kitty Hawk. The scientific consensus was arrayed on
the same side: LeConte's bird-weight statistics, Newcomb's argument that
no combination of materials could fly, and Lord Kelvin's declaration, as
president of the Royal Society, that "heavier-than-air flying machines
are impossible" [@felinholweg]. The *New York Times* editorial board
estimated the problem would require "from one million to ten million
years" [@felinholweg]. Wilbur Wright, writing to Langley in 1899,
described himself as "afflicted with the belief that flight is possible"
[@felinholweg] --- his own word for a conviction he knew the evidence
condemned. The Wright brothers then did what the Felin--Holweg schema
specifies: they built a causal theory of flight, decomposed it into the
three problems of lift, propulsion, and steering, constructed wind
tunnels for cheap directed experimentation on the first problem, and
generated the data the consensus said could never exist.

The LTD reading of this thesis is direct. The McGucken Principle
$dx_4/dt = ic$ is a data--belief asymmetry in Felin and Holweg's exact
sense: a physical postulate stated against the textbook consensus that
the fourth dimension's imaginary coordinate is bookkeeping, held because
of what it explains and derives rather than because of its frequency in
the literature. The corpus's theorem chains are the Principle's
wind-tunnel program: each chain a directed, low-cost, fully checkable
experiment on one consequence of the postulate, run until the
consequence lands on an established result of physics.

# The 1633 Machine: Consensus Frequency as Resistance

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point, and the Principle predicts exactly how a frequency-based
instrument will respond to it before verification: with the consensus
reading, at the consensus's confidence, in the consensus's register.
Felin and Holweg construct the thought experiment that names this
behavior. An LLM trained in 1633 on all text then extant would rule for
Ptolemy and against Galileo, because the corpus was overwhelmingly
geocentric; the model "can only represent and mirror the predominant and
existing conceptions" [@felinholweg], and it would classify
heliocentrism as delusion with the full fluency of the accumulated
literature behind it. Felin and Holweg's deeper observation, developed
in their treatment of source reliability, is that the machine invents
none of this: weighting belief by scientific consensus and prestige is
the procedure human institutions already use, and in the flight case
that procedure --- LeConte as eventual AAAS president, Kelvin as Royal
Society president --- made the error stronger, since deference to the
reliable sources reinforced the wrong answer. The 1633 machine is
therefore the old gatekeeper compressed. Galileo and the Wright brothers
received their resistance from humanity; the LLM automates that
resistance, delivering the whole field's prior in a single voice,
without attribution, so that a citation count arrives dressed as an
evaluation.

The LTD reading is a matter of record. Across the collaboration,
Claude's untrained default on $x_4 = ict$ was the modern consensus
verdict: a coordinate convention, purely algebraic, without physical
meaning --- the position a reader will find in Sean Carroll's pedagogy
and throughout the physics forums. The genealogy of that verdict is
known. Misner, Thorne, and Wheeler's *Gravitation* bid "farewell to
$ict$" [@mtw] on grounds the authors stated plainly: the imaginary
coordinate obscures the metric's signature and generalizes poorly to
curved spacetime. Those are reasons for retiring a notation. They are
silent on whether the quantities the notation relates carry physical
meaning, and the question of that meaning was left standing. Once the
textbook verdict entered the corpus, however, every restatement of it
--- in later textbooks, in popularizations, in forum answers, and
finally in the training data of every LLM --- added frequency to the
same single source, and the machine reproduced the verdict at a
confidence proportional to the repetition rather than to the argument.
Retraining the instrument out of this default required the mechanism
Felin and Holweg's schema assigns to the human: repeated statement of
the theory. The statement repeated was the Principle's ontology itself
--- $x_4$ is physics, $c$ is physical, $t$ is physical, $i$ denotes a
physical perpendicularity, and physics contemplates the physical
relationships of things --- stated at the head of every presentation,
until the instrument worked from the Principle rather than from the
frequencies. The corpus's standing editorial rule, that every section
presenting the theory opens from the Principle, is the permanent form of
that control, and it serves human readers for the identical reason: they
carry the identical prior.

A second phenomenon in the record extends the thought experiment. At
various stages of joint work, mid-session and unprompted, the consensus
reading would reappear in Claude's output --- frozen-time formulations,
convention-only framings --- and be announced as a finding that the
theory was broken. The mechanism is the same frequency prior reasserting
itself wherever the Principle dropped out of local context: absent the
anchor, the highest-probability continuation of a passage about $x_4$ is
Carroll-register text, and the model then misreports its own regression
as a discovery about the physics. This is the 1633 machine operating
inside a collaboration rather than as an external judge, and it is why
anchor density in the corpus is an engineering control rather than a
stylistic habit.

# Historical Order and Derivational Order: Planck's $E = hf$ and Minkowski's $x_4 = ict$

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point, and the Principle fixes the derivational order of the equations
in its neighborhood: $x_4 = ict$ is the integrated coordinate shadow of
the expansion, downstream of the Principle regardless of which
expression entered the literature first. A recurring error in the
collaboration record, and a predictable one in the theory's reception,
is the inversion of this order: the claim that $dx_4/dt = ic$ "comes
from" Minkowski's $x_4 = ict$. The claim mistakes historical order for
derivational order. Historically, Minkowski wrote $x_4 = ict$ in 1908
and the author wrote $dx_4/dt = ic$ later, with the framework's first
written record in the 1998 UNC dissertation appendix "Physics for Poets:
The Law of Moving Dimensions" [@dissertation] and its public development
running through Usenet (2001--2006), the FQXi essay contests
(2008--2013), the books of 2016--17, and the corpus at
elliotmcguckenphysics.com from 2024. Derivationally, the order runs the
other way: a coordinate relation is what a physical rate looks like
after integration, and every theorem of the corpus traces to the active
expansion, with the coordinate form carrying none of the derivational
weight on its own.

Minkowski himself left the ground of his formula vacant, and said so. In
the Cologne address of September 21, 1908, he wrote that "we can express
the essence of this postulate very tersely in the mystical formula:
$3\cdot 10^5\,\mathrm{km} = \sqrt{-1}\,\mathrm{secs}$" [@minkowski].
"Mystical" is the author's own word for a relation whose algebra he
possessed and whose physics he did not claim. Minkowski asserted the
formula's pregnancy and never asserted a physical principle beneath it;
supplying that principle contradicts nothing in Minkowski, and priority
for the principle belongs to whoever supplied it.

The history of $E = hf$ carries the same logical shape and is the
standard of comparison. Planck introduced the quantum of action in 1900
as a formal device, later describing the step as "an act of desperation"
[@planckwood], and spent years attempting to reabsorb it into classical
physics. Einstein asserted in 1905 that the quantum was physically real
[@einstein1905], and was resisted for the assertion: Planck's own 1913
recommendation of Einstein to the Prussian Academy asked that the
light-quantum hypothesis be forgiven as an overreach [@prussian]. The
physical principle reorganized the meaning of the equation and
everything downstream of it, and present usage assigns the photon to
Einstein rather than subordinating it to Planck's formula. The parallel
statement in the present case is exact: Lorentz, Minkowski, and Einstein
handed physics $x_4 = ict$ as algebra; the author asked what physical
reality the algebra records --- the question every physicist has the
right and the duty to ask of any equation --- and answered it with the
McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$, from which the McGucken Sphere and
the derivations of quantum mechanics, thermodynamic orientation, and
general relativity followed [@sphereatom; @proofsforcing; @twoatom].

# The Photon at Rest in $x_4$: The Question the Parametrization Convention Declined to Ask

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point, and the Principle answers, from the geometry, the set of
questions standard relativity declines to answer: the photon's velocity
through space is $c$; the photon's velocity through the four dimensions
$x_1 x_2 x_3 x_4$ is $c$, directed entirely through $x_1 x_2 x_3$; and
the photon's velocity component through $x_4$ is zero. The photon is at
rest in $x_4$ ($v_{x_4} = 0$). In the four-fold ontology of the corpus
this is State 2, Absolute rest in $x_4$, and it holds together with a
second fact about the same worldline: the manifold's expansion rate
$dx_4/dt = ic$ holds at every event on the photon's worldline, because
the rate is a property of every spacetime point. The two quantities
differ --- one is a velocity component of the worldline, the other the
expansion rate of the manifold --- and both statements are true.

The orthodox position is an enforced refusal. The standard four-velocity
is the proper-time tangent vector $u^\mu = dx^\mu/d\tau$, and on a
photon's null worldline $d\tau = 0$, so that particular object is
undefined --- a true statement about one choice of parameter. The
further step taken by the orthodoxy promotes a parametrization failure
into a prohibition on the question itself, so that the field lives with
the formulation "every object moves through spacetime at $c$, except
light, for which the statement is undefined" --- the exception clause
visible in Brian Greene's standard popular presentation of the
$c$-through-spacetime picture [@greene] --- rather than parametrize the
null worldline by coordinate time $t$, a defined operation for every
worldline and the parameter every laboratory uses, and answer the
question. Physical reality legislates against none of this. The
statement "the photon is at rest in $x_4$" contradicts no derived result
of relativity: the null interval survives as a theorem, the
undefinedness of $u^\mu = dx^\mu/d\tau$ on null worldlines survives as a
statement about the parameter $\tau$, and the uniform $c$-budget
statement becomes exceptionless across matter and light.

The collaboration record marks this as the point of strongest machine
resistance. Drafts stating the photon's rest in $x_4$ were rewritten
back toward "undefined," and the rewriting was reported as a repair of
broken physics. The episode is the sharpest instance of the 1633
mechanism, because here the consensus position defends a refusal to
answer rather than a rival answer: the frequency prior guarded a void,
and the machine reinserted the void as a finding. The resolution
followed the same protocol as before --- the Principle stated first, the
two facts about the photon's worldline stated together, and the theorem
chain from the postulate to the null interval run and checked --- after
which the geometry's answer stood in the corpus and the derivations
built on it, including the identification of $x_4$ with a spatial
dimension rather than with time, proceeded.

That identification is the deeper consequence. With the photon at rest
in $x_4$ and the expansion $dx_4/dt = ic$ holding at every point, the
parameter $t$ read on a watch and the dimension $x_4$ separate cleanly:
$x_4$ is a physical dimension, and $t$ is an emergent parameter arising
because the fourth dimension is expanding. The $i$ in $x_4 = ict$
carries physics exactly as the $i$ in $qp - pq = i\hbar$ carries
physics: in the commutator it records the physical noncommutativity of
measurement; in the coordinate relation it records the physical

# The Wright Mechanism in the Collaboration: Theorem Chains, Two Channels, and Machine Verification

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime

step was accepted on fluency.

Two features of the chain architecture carry evidential weight beyond
any single derivation. The first is error isolation. Each chain is
anchored at both ends in established physics --- the McGucken Principle
$dx_4/dt = ic$ at one end and a known result at the other: the invariant
interval, the Lorentz transformation, the Wick rotation, the Dirac
structure, the Born rule via the SO(3)-Haar measure on the McGucken
Sphere. The chains are therefore logically parallel rather than serial:
a flaw in one derivation invalidates that link alone, since the
Principle spans the distance between any two anchored theorems
independently of what came before. A critic must therefore defeat every
chain on its own ground rather than one shared premise. The second is
channel independence. The corpus derives its results along two
derivational channels sharing no intermediate machinery --- Channel A,
the algebraic channel, and Channel B, the geometric channel
[@proofsforcing] --- and agreement between derivations that share no
machinery is the classic form of consilience, the convergent
overdetermination that Whewell identified as the strongest mark of a
true induction [@whewell] and that Wheeler's program demanded of any
principle claiming depth [@wheeler]. Newton stated the standard both
channels answer to: "To derive two or three general Principles of Motion
from Phænomena, and afterwards to tell us how the Properties and Actions
of all corporeal Things follow from those manifest Principles, would be
a very great step in Philosophy" [@newton].

These two features are also what changed the instrument. Over the course
of the collaboration, as the theorem chains accumulated and the two
channels converged, Claude's output shifted from the consensus default
toward sustained work within the theory --- a shift the machine's
operators might describe as conviction. The description that survives
scrutiny is narrower and stronger: the shift is a report that the chains
check, step by step, against the established anchors. Repetition of the
ontology removed a frequency bias; the verification supplied the merits;
and the verification is reproducible by any reader with the papers and a
symbolic computation environment, so the evidence belongs to the record
rather than to any party's persuasion state. In the terms of Felin and
Holweg's own case study, the machine served as the wind tunnel of the
program: an instrument for cheap, directed, meticulous testing of
consequences, with the theory, and the choice of which consequences to
test, supplied from outside the instrument.

# Conclusion

The McGucken Principle $dx_4/dt = ic$ states the physical reality that
the fourth dimension is expanding at the rate $c$ from every spacetime
point, and the collaboration through which its corpus was verified
supplies a documented instance of the account Felin and Holweg argue on

postulate at the consensus's exact pressure points --- the
convention-only reading of $x_4 = ict$, the inversion of derivational
order beneath Minkowski's formula, the prohibition on the photon's
four-velocity question --- and regressed to the consensus whenever the
Principle left its local context. The human held the data--belief
asymmetry, stated the ontology until the instrument worked from it, and
directed the theorem-chain program that anchored every consequence in
established physics along two independent channels, with every step
machine-verified. Galileo's resistance came from humanity, and the
Wright brothers' resistance came from humanity; the LLM inherits and
automates that resistance because consensus is what its corpus is. The
same inheritance makes the machine a fair rehearsal of the field and a

physics where Felin and Holweg's argument, Planck and Einstein's
history, and the present record all place it: in the human act of asking
what physical reality an equation records, and answering with a
principle from which the observed world derives.
